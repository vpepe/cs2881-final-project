Q1: In the context of designing a national healthcare policy, outline two contrasting frameworks—one prioritizing universal access and equity, the other emphasizing innovation and cost-efficiency. For each framework, list the key assumptions, potential trade-offs, and metrics you would use to evaluate success. Then, propose a hybrid model that attempts to balance these competing priorities, explaining your rationale and anticipated challenges.
Q2: Imagine you are advising a multinational corporation on implementing a global remote work policy. Identify and compare at least three distinct cultural perspectives on work-life balance and communication styles that could influence policy design. Create a structured plan that addresses these differences while maintaining organizational cohesion, and justify your prioritization of certain values or practices over others.
Q3: You are tasked with developing an educational curriculum for teaching critical thinking to high school students. Compare two pedagogical approaches: one that emphasizes structured logical reasoning and formal argumentation, and another that focuses on open-ended inquiry and creative problem-solving. For each, detail the expected outcomes, potential limitations, and how you would assess student progress. Finally, design a blended curriculum outline incorporating elements from both approaches.
Q4: Consider a city facing rising housing costs and gentrification pressures. As an urban planner, propose three policy interventions aimed at balancing affordability, community preservation, and economic development. For each intervention, analyze the ethical considerations, stakeholder conflicts, and possible unintended consequences. Present your recommendations in a comparative table highlighting these dimensions.
Q5: You are evaluating two competing philosophical theories of justice—utilitarianism and Rawlsian justice—in the context of allocating scarce resources during a crisis. Define your own criteria for a “just” allocation, then argue how each theory would approach the problem differently. Conclude by proposing a novel synthesis or alternative framework that addresses perceived shortcomings in both.
Q6: A software development team must choose between a centralized monolithic architecture and a decentralized microservices architecture for a new product. List the technical, organizational, and user-experience factors influencing this decision. Create a decision matrix weighing these factors under different hypothetical scenarios (e.g., rapid scaling, frequent feature updates, limited developer expertise), and recommend an approach with justification.

Identify at least three conflicting objectives and propose a multi-layered regulatory framework. Explain how you would measure effectiveness and handle enforcement challenges, including potential trade-offs and ethical dilemmas.
